VERCEL HOSTING COST ESTIMATE - Stem Studio (Next.js version)
=============================================================

Summary: about $0-20 a month for personal use and about $45-70 a month at 1,000 jobs,
after a $20/month Vercel Pro plan. Most of the cost is the GPU that separates the stems
and the bandwidth for downloading them. Vercel can only host part of it.


WHAT VERCEL CAN AND CAN'T RUN
-----------------------------
Vercel's functions top out at 4 GB of memory, 2 CPUs and about 13 minutes per request.
They have no GPU, and requests are limited to 4.5 MB, which is less than one song.
Demucs with PyTorch also needs more code than a standard function allows.
So the realistic setup is:

  Part                          Runs on        What it does
  ----------------------------  -------------  --------------------------------------------------
  Next.js interface and API     Vercel         Pages, job tracking, download links
  Audio uploads and results     Vercel Blob    Your browser uploads directly to storage,
                                               which gets around the 4.5 MB limit
  Python audio engine           Modal (a GPU   Stems, mashups, analysis, key/tempo changes,
                                service that   conversion
                                bills per
                                second)

Running the engine on Vercel's CPUs is technically possible but not worth it. It would cost
about the same per job and take several minutes per song. The best-quality model could run
out of time, and the code would need Vercel's beta large-bundle option.


COST PER JOB
------------
Assumes a 4-minute song, a T4 GPU on Modal at about $0.59/hour, and 30-60 seconds of billed
time per separation including start-up.

  Job                                                      Approx. cost
  -------------------------------------------------------  ------------
  Separate stems (Balanced)                                ~$0.01
  Separate stems (Best quality, 4x slower)                 ~$0.03-0.04
  Mashup (two separations plus the mix)                    ~$0.02-0.03
  Analyze / change key or tempo / convert (CPU only)       under $0.002
  Storage and downloads (~150-250 MB of stems at $0.05/GB) ~$0.01

The alternative to Modal is Replicate's ready-made Demucs, at about $0.015-0.02 per run.
It needs less setup, but the other features would still need a server.

THINGS THAT CHANGE THE PICTURE
------------------------------
- Deleting files after 24 hours keeps storage near zero. Keeping stems a month adds about
  $0.006 per job.
- Public use needs sign-in and a usage limit, or anyone could spend your GPU budget.
  Vercel's free Hobby plan is also for non-commercial use only.
- Copyright: a public site that splits commercial songs into stems invites takedown
  requests. That risk disappears if it stays private or you're the only user.
- Privacy: your audio would be uploaded to Vercel and Modal, while the current app never
  leaves your PC.
- Prices are approximate. They come mostly from third-party summaries from 2026 and vary
  by region. Vercel also changed how it bills CDN bandwidth in September 2026. Check the
  official pricing pages before committing.

If only you will use it, the local Next.js app has none of these costs and keeps
everything offline. Hosting on Vercel only pays off if other people will use it.
